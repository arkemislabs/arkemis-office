Paragraph border preset/toggle behavior translated to JavaScript from WordCraft commit 3de6ff95af86c19d67030cd96dba15cd6a45a25e, crates/engine/src/cmd/para/tabs_borders.rs and crates/engine/src/cmd/para.rs; border fields follow crates/doc/src/props/para_props.rs. ProseMirror selection adapter, validation and OOXML/UI integration are original adaptations. Browser rendering supports the four outer sides with solid/double/dotted/dashed lines. Between-paragraph borders and additional source line styles are preserved in native content and DOCX but their exact Word layout is not rendered by the browser. External hyperlink insertion also adapts crates/engine/src/cmd/insert/links.rs: selected ranges retain marks; a collapsed caret inserts the supplied label or URL, then clears pending link styling. Internal bookmarks/heading destinations are not translated. No Rust or WASM is used.
